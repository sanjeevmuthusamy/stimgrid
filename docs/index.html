stimgrid

Fit a stimulation pulse to what a stimulator can actually produce: its time steps, current steps and limits. stimgrid rounds the pulse to the device's grid and finds the nearest pulse whose two phases carry exactly the same charge. This page runs the real stimgrid package inside your browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Device
Aim

List pulses the device produces exactly and that are exactly balanced, closest to this aim first.

A ratio of 4 makes the second phase 4 times longer and 4 times weaker; 1 is symmetric. Leave the first width empty to search every width.
